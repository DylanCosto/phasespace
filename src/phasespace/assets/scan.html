Reading this scan

Results cover the included calls only. Unknown intervals may hide further variants, and a reference protein sequence does not establish function. No phase probabilities, population frequencies or pathogenicity are assessed. “No reference protein” often reflects common homozygous variants. PhaseSpace does not perform phasing and does not make a diagnosis.
