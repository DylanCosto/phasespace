    Reading this report

    Each arrangement is one way the called heterozygous alleles could be distributed between the two gene copies. Predictions cover the included calls only. Unknown intervals may hide further variants. A reference-identical CDS does not establish protein function, and no phase probabilities are assigned. PhaseSpace does not perform phasing and does not make a diagnosis.
